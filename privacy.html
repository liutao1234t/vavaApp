Our AI is ours. Your privacy stays yours.

WHY & HOW LONG

Use and retention

Purposes

  • Provide discovery, search, recommendations, favorites, community, messaging, and virtual try-on.
  • Authenticate accounts, remember settings, deliver notifications you enable, and answer support requests.
  • Detect abuse, protect users, diagnose failures, and maintain performance.
  • Comply with valid legal obligations and enforce our agreements.
  • Improve our own models and product only with data handled under applicable law and appropriate controls; where consent is required, we ask first.

Legal bases

Depending on your location, processing is based on performing our contract with you, your consent, our legitimate interests in security and service improvement, or compliance with law. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the stated purpose. Temporary AI processing inputs may be removed after generation unless you choose to save them. Account content remains while your account is active or until deleted. Limited records may remain for backups, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, safety, or legal requirements, then are securely deleted or de-identified.

ACCESS & SAFEGUARDS

Who can reach information

Authorized Vava personnel may access the minimum information needed for support, safety, security, and operations under confidentiality and access controls. We do not share user information with advertisers, data brokers, other users, or outside AI model providers.

Technical infrastructure vendors may process encrypted or limited data strictly on our behalf when essential to host or deliver Vava. They are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. This controlled processing is not a sale, trade, rental, or sharing of user information. If a binding legal demand requires disclosure, we assess it narrowly and notify you when legally allowed.

International processing

Information may be processed where Vava operates infrastructure. When data crosses borders, we use legally recognized safeguards and maintain the protections described here.

Security

We use data minimization, role-based access, secure transmission, protected storage, logging, testing, and incident response. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach creates legally reportable risk, we will notify affected users and authorities as required.

Your choices and rights

Subject to local law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection; withdraw consent; or appeal a privacy decision. You may also manage photo, camera, and notification permissions in device settings. Email support@Vava.help. We may verify your request to protect your account.

Children

Vava is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Higher age or consent thresholds apply where required. Contact us if you believe a child provided information.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy as features or laws change. We will update the effective date and provide prominent notice for material changes.

QUICK ANSWERS

FAQ

Does Vava sell or share my information?

No. Vava does not sell, trade, rent, or share user information. Essential vendors may process limited data only under Vava’s instructions to operate the service.

No. Our AI models are developed, trained, and deployed by us; we do not use third-party AI model services.

Can I ask Vava to delete my data?

Yes. Use in-app controls where available or email support@Vava.help. Limited records may remain where law, safety, security, or backups require it.

Does Vava use face recognition?

No. Portraits are used for the requested visual try-on, not to identify who you are.

Questions deserve a human answer.

For privacy, account, licensing, safety, or legal questions, email the Vava support team. Please do not include sensitive information you do not need us to see.
